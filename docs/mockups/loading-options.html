PLATTER · LOADING STATES

Five ways to show that a view or a card is still waiting for data. All of them are moving on this page.

1 · Logo spinner RECOMMENDED

The Slice logo with its blue piece travelling around the disk. Recognisably Platter; for a card or a whole view that is waiting.

Reading /home…

2 · Placeholder blocks RECOMMENDED

Grey blocks in the shape of the view, pulsing gently. The layout does not jump when the data arrives. Best when a whole view opens.

3 · Ring

The standard turning ring. Everyone understands it, but it looks like every other app.

Loading…

4 · Map blocks

Three tiles of the Explorer map lighting up in turn. Fits the Explorer; less clear elsewhere.

Building the map…

5 · Line on top

The old content stays visible but dimmed, with a thin moving line on the top edge. Best for a refresh, when there is already something to show.

When each is used

My suggestion for the real app:

Under 0.3 s: show nothing, so fast views do not flicker.
A view opens: placeholder blocks (2).
One card waits, such as opening a big folder or loading the model: logo spinner (1) with a line saying what is happening.
Rescan or refresh: line on top (5) over the old data.
Full disk scan: the existing counter and progress bar, since it has a real percentage.
